# Scuderia "CòScienza" 🧠⚛️

Welcome to the GitHub organization of **Scuderia "CòScienza"**, a theoretical and computational research group based at the **Department of Basic and Applied Sciences for Engineering (SBAI)**, **Sapienza University of Rome**.

---

### 🔬 About Our Lab

In our lab, we apply quantitative methods rooted in the **Statistical Mechanics of Disordered Systems**, combining rigorous analytical formulations with large-scale computational simulations (we are a *"spin-glass group"*).

Our primary research focus is uncovering the **emergent information-processing capabilities of modern neural architectures**, while also tackling complex problems in **quantitative biology** and **condensed matter physics**.

---

### 🧭 Research Lines & Topics

Our current investigations span theoretical physics, mathematical physics, and computational neuroscience:

* **Associative Neural Networks & Storage Capacity:** Exponential storage in generalized Hebbian networks, dense associative memories, and sleeping/unlearning dynamics.
* **Neural Substrates of Spatial Navigation:** Attractor network models of grid and place cells for cognitive mapping and path integration.
* **Pattern Disentanglement:** Multidirectional associative models, cross-modal learning, and structured representation extraction.
* **Emergent Complexity & Parallel Processing:** Interaction dynamics in networks of neural networks and extensive parallel computing.
* **First-Passage Phenomena & Transport:** Random walks and diffusive transport on complex, fractal, and inhomogeneous networks.
* **Biological Computing & Reaction Kinetics:** Quantitative information processing via chemical and biochemical reaction networks.
* **Ultrametric Fluctuations in Biological Matter:** Energy landscapes, replica symmetry breaking, and ultrametricity in highly homologous protein families.
