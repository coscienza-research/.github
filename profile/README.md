Welcome to the official GitHub organization of **Scuderia "Cò'Scienza"** (also known as the *Agliari–Barra Lab*), a theoretical and computational research group based at the **Department of Basic and Applied Sciences for Engineering (SBAI)** and the **Department of Mathematics "Guido Castelnuovo"**, **Sapienza University of Rome**.

---

### 🔬 About Our Lab

In our laboratory, we apply rigorous quantitative methods rooted in the **Statistical Mechanics of Disordered Systems** (we are historically a *"spin-glass group"*), bridging mathematical physics, analytical mechanics, and high-performance computing.

Our primary research focus is uncovering the **emergent information-processing capabilities of modern neural architectures**, memory retrieval dynamics, and machine learning principles, alongside investigations into **quantitative biology**, **reaction kinetics**, and **condensed matter physics**.

---

### 🧭 Research Pillars & Lines of Investigation

Our active research lines encompass theoretical physics, mathematical modeling, and computational neuroscience:

* 🧠 **Associative Neural Networks & Storage Capacity**  
  Exponential storage capacity in generalized Hebbian networks, dense associative memories (DAM / modern Hopfield networks with multi-body interactions), and multi-layer hetero-associative architectures.
* 💤 **Dreaming Dynamics & Small-Dataset Regimes**  
  Statistical mechanics of sleep and dreaming in artificial neural networks. Mimicking biological unlearning and reinforcement to enable robust pattern extraction, denoising, and generalisation even under severe data scarcity (saving up to 90% of training data requirements).
* 🔀 **Pattern Disentanglement & Multi-Modal Binding**  
  Decomposing composite/spurious signals and unravelling mixed representations via multi-directional associative architectures, cross-modal learning, and thermodynamic binding of chimeric states.
* 🧭 **Neural Substrates of Spatial Navigation**  
  Continuous attractor network models and statistical-mechanical formulations of place cells, grid cells, and path integration in cognitive mapping.
* 🌐 **Networks of Neural Networks & Distributed / Federated Learning**  
  Interactions and emergent collective dynamics in ensembles of interconnected neural networks, extensively parallel computing, and data-quality-calibrated federated associative systems.
* 📶 **Signal-to-Noise Tradeoffs & Weak-Signal Detection**  
  Dense neural networks capable of dynamically tuning signal-to-noise thresholds, sacrificing memory storage capacity to detect faint signals deeply submerged in noise.
* 🧬 **Quantitative Biology, Reaction Kinetics & Ultrametricity**  
  Information processing via biochemical reaction networks, replica symmetry breaking (RSB) and ultrametric energy landscapes in highly homologous protein families.
* 🕸️ **Transport & First-Passage Phenomena on Complex Graphs**  
  Diffusion, random walks, and exact Laplacian spectra on fractal, hierarchical, and inhomogeneous networks (e.g., Berker–Ostlund diamond lattices).

### 💻 Open Source Scientific Software & Toolkits

This GitHub space hosts the open, reproducible codebases accompanying our research papers:
* **Disordered Systems Engines:** Fast Monte Carlo simulation pipelines, cavity field solvers, and replica-symmetric/RSB numerical evaluation algorithms.
* **Modern Associative Networks:** PyTorch and CUDA implementations for multi-layer hetero-associative networks, dense associative memories (DAM), and multidirectional Hopfield models.
* **Dreaming & Unlearning Algorithms:** Implementations of sleep-inspired consolidation and pattern disentanglement routines for small-dataset learning.
* **Spectral Analysis on Graphs:** Analytical solvers and numerical algorithms for graph Laplacians on fractal and inhomogeneous networks.
